GAME MECHANICS

⚔️ KNIGHTS

Buy a Knight with wool, wheat and ore. Play it on a later turn to move the robber. Three played Knights can earn Largest Army (+2 VP).

♟ ROBBER

Rolling a 7 triggers discards for players holding more than seven cards. Move the robber to block a tile and possibly steal a random resource from an adjacent opponent.

🛤️ LONGEST ROAD

Build a continuous road of at least five segments to qualify for Longest Road (+2 VP).

HOW TO WIN

Gather resources, build settlements and cities, trade with opponents, and reach 10 victory points.
